## 参考与变量

唯一脸部主参考：F:/天命之子/codex专区/analysis/style-references/face-audit-2026-09-14/01-dc-faces-5.png（原始裁切165×175像素，来自300_libstats_103.png）。不是高分辨率原画，不从放大图推断不存在的笔触细节。

## 结果检查

v001眼睛、嘴形和鼻光相对参考放大，头顶被裁切。v002修订后眼睛更窄、嘴形更短，构图拉远并露出肩部；皇冠区域仍接触上边缘，未完全实现上方留白。鼻光仍偏大偏硬、下侧鼻线仍明显，虹膜和睫毛的细节密度高于低分辨率参考可支持的程度。不能因表情变冷或闭嘴就声称学到了DC画风。不同视角和裁切使严格的像素尺寸比较无效，本次为人工视觉检查，没有相似度评分。

Selective thin reddish-brown facial contours and clearly drawn eyelids integrated into smooth painted volume. Crisp at lids, hair overlaps and small nose light; softer at turning cheek planes. ## 修订完整提示词

参考1控制结构；参考2只是待修改的初稿，脸部几何明确允许改变。

~~~text
Facial construction calibration, one finished square head-and-shoulders portrait of an adult 28-year-old woman.
Image 1 is the authoritative ORIGINAL STYLE AND FACIAL STRUCTURE reference from Destiny Child.
Image 2 is the EDIT TARGET, an imperfect previous study. Preserve its ink-blue short hair identity, ivory hair clasp, teal eyes, matte navy collar and smooth warm skin palette. Its current facial geometry is NOT locked: correct it from image 1.

Targeted corrections:
- Make eye openings shallower to match image 1. Reduce vertical eye opening by about one quarter relative to image 2, while preserving width and the reference's angled upper lids. The upper lids should cover more of each iris; do not just shrink irises inside unchanged round eye shapes. Keep the deliberate low brow/eyelid relationship, farther eye perspective and partial diagonal fringe overlap.
- Match the smaller mouth-to-face scale in image 1: shorten the mouth's width relative to image 2 and keep it almost horizontal, naturally closed and serious. Small muted lip color, minimal single light accent; no smile, pout or wide glossy lip outline.
- Reduce the nose bright shape to a compact small plane like image 1, substantially smaller than image 2's large light teardrop. Keep it integrated with a short understated nose; remove the long dark nose contour. This is a scale/shape correction, not just reducing brightness.
- Preserve mid-cheek width and continuous cheek-to-jaw taper from original reference. Keep a restrained chin point, not a long sharp V or sculpted supermodel cheekbones.
- Pull framing back enough to include complete hair crown with a little background margin and both shoulder tops; face still large and clear. Do not crop the head.

Finish follows image 1's clean colored shapes and selectively modeled volume: calm cheek planes, soft transitions at cheeks with crisp eyelids and compact cast shadows. No rough brushgrain. Keep hair's broad ink-blue groups with only selected broad cool highlights. Plain warm-gray background, no props or text. Correct reference-matching geometry first; do not add makeup or ornamental details.
~~~
